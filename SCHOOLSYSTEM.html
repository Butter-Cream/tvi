♱ Student Council ♱

Student Council is the only official club ran by the school in TVI. It is a group of the best and dedicated students from every year elected to contribute and help school. Members work together with school staff to improve school life, organise events and create a perfect study place.

The Council encourages leadership, teamwork and responsibility while giving students a chance to make real changes in their school community.

The club members are higher in hierarchy than normal students, giving them greater power while at school grounds and in school-related matters. They are commonly respected by most of the people due to Student Council being limited to certain type of students.
