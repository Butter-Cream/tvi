✦ 𓂃 More information about the roleplay 𓂃 ✦

Dark Essence (DE) is a "magical" powerful substance discovered over 9 000 years ago, before The Explosion happened. It was seen as the new discovery that will lead humankind into a greater future, but due to war and the urgent need for better weaponary scientists' work became fast and unstable, soon leading to the terrible incident. Nowadays it can be used in many various ways: weapons, transportation - fuel and in medicine, but it also can be used in a negative way as signal breaks, bombs, poison, or even drugs. Most of the people cannot acces it and it's exclusively only available to high-rank people, such as scientists, doctors, royal families and anybody that gets permission to use it.

What many people don't think about is about how DE affected, and in fact, still affects their daily life. DE was affecting everybody's lives throughout the centuries, changing both their bodies and mind, as well as everything around them. The physical change is rather obvious - it changes on environment, people can posses animalistic, nonhuman traits, adapting to their style of life. The nonphysical, mental change is subtle, almost not visible - people can be more agressive, greedy, narcissistic and generally feel less empathy for others. Nowadays it is a common trait to think mostly about yourself or personal-related situations, thus nobody thinks about it like decades before.
